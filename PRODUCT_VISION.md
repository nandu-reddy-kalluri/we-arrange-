# YMWA — Product Vision

## 1. High-Level Vision Statement
To establish the ultimate premium, human-curated marketplace and planning platform for Indian luxury weddings, starting with Hyderabad's elite venues and vendors.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions
- **Eliminate Curation Chaos:** No automated, outdated database listings. Every partner is vetted and personally contacted for fresh quotations.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Compare venues and vendor packages in a clean, unified dashboard matrix.
- **Bespoke Digital Utilities:** Real-time editable invitations, responsive custom subdomains, guest photo dump portals, and RSVP managers.

---

## 3. Product Roadmap
- **Phase 1 MVP (Current):** Landing page, requirements form, concierge details presentation, initial design systems, and placeholder routes.
- **Phase 2 Integration:** Live customer planning dashboards, admin portal integration, Supabase backend hookup, database mapping.
- **Phase 3 Curation Engine:** Advanced comparison sheets, vendor portfolios builder, digital invitation editing engines, subdomains deployment manager.
