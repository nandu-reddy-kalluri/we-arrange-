# YMWA Skill Guide: Wedding Domain Glossary

This document outlines key domain definitions and business classifications for YouMarriageWeArrange.

---

## 1. Core Terminology
- **Venue Categories:** Heritage palaces, luxury resorts, five-star banquets, premium farmhouses, and outdoor lawns.
- **Vendor Roles:** Photographic artists, bespoke decors designers, elite caterers, makeup specialists, and coordinates planners.
- **Shortlist Cart:** A curation bucket where users add venues and vendors for coordination reviews.

## 2. Business Specifications
- **Catering Models:**
  - *In-house:* Catering is supplied exclusively by the venue.
  - *External Allowed:* Venue allows clients to bring external catering.
- **Pricing Indicators:** Express pricing ranges in lakhs (e.g. `₹18L Onwards`, `₹45L Onwards`) to align with standard Hyderabad premium weddings vocabulary.
- **Capacity Range:** Enclose capacity indicators (e.g. `200 - 1,200 Guests`) to evaluate search criteria.
